About

What is Check4Spam?

Check4Spam is a free fact-checking service dedicated to identifying and exposing fake news, hoaxes, and misinformation circulating on social media. Founded in Bengaluru, India, our mission is simple — make life easy for everyone, and make life tough for spammers.

Created by tech enthusiasts Shammas Oliyath and Bal Krishn Birla, Check4Spam has grown into a trusted, community-driven platform that helps you decide if a viral message is true or false. We respond to hundreds of verification requests every day.

Origin Story

How Elections 2014 Inspired It All

In 2014, during the Indian General Elections, witnessing firsthand how misinformation and viral messages influenced public opinion sparked the idea for Check4Spam — a platform dedicated to fact-checking and helping people separate truth from fake forwards.

A sense of social responsibility combined with a passion for technology was the catalyst. Read the full personal journey on Medium.

Process

How It Works

01
Submit a Forward or Claim
Send the suspicious message to our WhatsApp number or via our online submission form. Fast, simple, and accessible to everyone.
02
We Investigate
Our team conducts thorough research, verifying data with trusted sources and through detailed fact-checking methodology.
03
Spam or Not Spam?
We clearly label the claim as NOT SPAM (true) or SPAM (false) and provide a detailed, evidence-backed report.
04
See the Proof
Every verification post includes evidence: source links, images, and official statements — with regular updates as new info emerges.
